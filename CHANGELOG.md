# Changelog

## 1.6.0 — 2026-08-15

**Improved**
- Smoothed out the timing on one of the app's easter eggs.
- The feedback button now waits until you've spent a little time exploring the app before it appears, instead of showing up right away.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-08-09

**New**
- A new easter egg to find.

**Improved**
- Made a couple of easter eggs feel smoother to use.
- Made a small on-screen detail easier to interact with.
- Fixed a small visual hiccup in the background photos.
- The app loads a little faster thanks to smaller image files.
- The reset option now puts everything back to how it started, not just part of it.

**Security**
- Strengthened the feedback form against malicious input, keeping submissions safe.

**Behind the scenes**
- Routine maintenance and code tidying.

## 1.4.0 — 2026-08-08

**New**
- Another easter egg to stumble onto while playing with the machine.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-08-04

**Improved**
- Clearer, more honest counting of how many easter eggs you've found, including what does and doesn't count toward the total.
- Fixed the easter egg counter so it can no longer overcount.
- Fixed some visual timing issues so things line up more naturally.
- Fixed the machine's layout on short, landscape phone screens.
- The version number on the machine's nameplate now shows the build time as well.

**Behind the scenes**
- Switched the project's internal tooling a couple of times and sped up automated testing substantially.

**Improved**

**New**

## 0.0.1 — 2026-07-25 to 2026-08-03

**New**
- The core idea: recreating the classic "useless machine" and questioning its premise.
- A growing set of easter eggs to stumble onto while playing with the machine, plus a collection view to keep track of what you've found and a way to start fresh if you want to hunt for them again.
- A feedback button so you can tell the makers what you think.

**Improved**
- Many rounds of tuning how the arm moves and reacts, and how the switch feels to press and drag.

**Behind the scenes**
- Set up the project's automated testing and deployment pipeline.
