# Changelog

Plain-language notes on what's changed in Useless Machine — written for
anyone, not just developers. Newest changes at the top. This log never
explains how to find a hidden surprise or what it does; if you stumble onto
one, that discovery is the whole point.

## Unreleased

Nothing yet.

## 0.0.1 — 2026-07-25 to 2026-08-03

The early build. Most of the app's personality took shape during this stretch.

**New**
- The core toy: a switch that turns on, then a little arm reaches in and switches it back off — the whole joke of the app.
- Redesigned the machine to look like a metal plate mounted on the wall, screwed down, with hand-labeled controls.
- The plate can be opened up, screw by screw, to reveal more of the machine underneath.
- A growing set of hidden surprises to stumble onto while playing with the switch and the plate, plus a collection view to keep track of what you've found and a way to start fresh if you want to hunt for them again.
- A feedback button so you can tell the makers what you think.

**Improved**
- Many rounds of tuning how the arm moves and reacts, and how the switch feels to press and drag.

**Behind the scenes**
- Set up the project's automated testing and deployment pipeline.
