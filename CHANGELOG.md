# Changelog

Plain-language notes on what's changed in Useless Machine — written for
anyone, not just developers. Newest changes at the top. This log never
explains how to find a hidden surprise or what it does; if you stumble onto
one, that discovery is the whole point.

## Unreleased

Nothing yet.

## 1.6.0 — 2026-08-15

**Improved**
- The feedback button now waits until you've spent a little time exploring the app before it appears, instead of showing up right away.
- Smoothed out the timing on one of the app's hidden surprises.
- Made one of the app's playful hidden interactions feel smoother to use.
- Made a small on-screen detail easier to interact with.
- Fixed a small visual hiccup when swiping through the background photos.
- The reset option now puts everything back to how it started, not just part of it.
- The app loads a little faster thanks to smaller image files.

**Security**
- Strengthened the feedback form against malicious input, keeping submissions safe.

**Behind the scenes**
- Routine maintenance and code tidying to keep the project running smoothly.
